    Who we are?

    Welcome to Lumen Orientale Media Productions!

    We are a creative production studio based in Norfolk, United Kingdom, dedicated to telling powerful and authentic stories through the cameras, media and photography.

    We work and collaborate with various Roman Catholic organizations, communities, and individuals to capture stories of faith, mission, vocation, service, and human experience.

    Our main goal is simple: To illuminate stories of faith, hope, beauty, and humanity through exceptional film and photography, serving the Catholic Church while inspiring audiences around the world.


    About our founder

    Chavez Cyrillus Mariae Poon

    Chavez is the founder and creative director of Lumen Orientale Media Productions.

    With a passion for storytelling and a deep commitment to the Catholic faith, Chavez has dedicated his career to creating compelling visual narratives that inspire and uplift audiences around the world.

    Our works and projects

    We create compelling visual stories that help communities, ministries, and mission-driven organizations share their faith, hope, and purpose with the world.

    From documentary filmmaking to photography and media production, each project is crafted to illuminate the beauty of human experience, spiritual life, and the transformative power of Christ.
